# Independent source and redistribution-rights review

**Reviewed 1 November 2026. This is research, not legal advice or a license grant. The corpus is not cleared for public website, APK, commercial use, or relicensing.** The original source/translator/permission statements remain in `data/legacy-attribution.md` and `data/claims.json` as historical **claims**. Six comparison-source records in `data/sources.json` have `unverified` status; none grants an allowed use. `npm run check:publication` intentionally fails until the exact text, edition, rightsholder, territories and uses are reviewed and documented.

## Before release

1. Inventory the **exact** Arabic, Persian and English edition for each row or paragraph, including additions, editorial changes and any inherited notes. Do not assign a publisher or translator solely because a normalized string matched a reference.
2. For every protected edition obtain written, attributable permission covering website and offline Android distribution, modification/correction, attribution, territories and any commercial or advertising use. Keep evidence and required notices with the release. Replace or omit content where authority cannot be demonstrated.
3. Separate independent translator rights, edition rights, database terms, source-code licenses, trademarks and moral rights. Ancient Arabic texts being old does not clear modern translations or edited transcriptions.
4. Record the exact source, license/permission, allowed uses, evidence URL or grant and attribution in `data/sources.json`, attach it at the correct item/row/document/paragraph level, and obtain qualified content and alignment review.
5. Run `npm run check:publication` for the actual target uses. A successful structural build or a private audit archive is not publication approval. Jurisdiction-specific questions and any purported conflict between named license and additional conditions require competent legal review.
