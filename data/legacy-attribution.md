# CREDITS — sources & rights (v1.0.0)

Arabic texts of du'as/ziyarat are classical public-domain works. What follows covers
**translations, editions, and modern texts**. The user's standard: copyright-clean
*religiously*, not just legally — keep these credits in the app's about screen.

## English blocks
- erfan.ir / Al-Hassanain (non-commercial permissioned use, credit both).
  NOTE: the erfan.ir English edition (via mafatih-al-jinan.com JSON) leaves most **prayer bodies untranslated**
  (Arabic-only inserts, "say the following words:"); those blocks carry merits, introductions and ritual instructions.
- **AhlulBayt_Treasures** (github.com/KazTronics/AhlulBayt_Treasures, `duas_data.jsonl`) — row-level English
  translations of prayer bodies (duas.org-derived community translations, non-commercial use with credit).
  Blocks with `translator: "AhlulBayt_Treasures"` / id prefix `tr` carry the full body text.
- duas.org (Nad Ali page: transliteration + translation, credit).
- en.wikishia (CC BY-SA, credit) where used.
